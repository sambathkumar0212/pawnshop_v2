How to Create a WhatsApp Broadcast List
1
Open WhatsApp on your phone
Go to the WhatsApp app (not WhatsApp Web — broadcast lists are created on the phone app)
2
Tap New Broadcast
On Android: Tap ⋮ (3 dots) → "New Broadcast"
On iPhone: Tap "Broadcast Lists" at the top left → "New List"
3
Add contacts from the list below
Search for each contact using the phone numbers shown on the right and add them to the broadcast list. Save the list as "Campaign Leads" or any name you prefer.
4
Done! Now go to Step 2
Once the broadcast list is created, proceed to Step 2 to download your flyer and send it to all contacts in one tap.
Important: Contacts must have saved your number in their phone to receive broadcast messages. Ask them to save {{ organization_name }}'s number first.

How to Send Flyer to Your Broadcast List
1
Download or Copy the flyer image
Click "Download Flyer" to save the image to your device, or "Copy Image" to copy it directly to clipboard.
2
Open your Broadcast List in WhatsApp
On Android: ⋮ → Broadcast Lists → select your "Campaign Leads" list
On iPhone: Tap "Broadcast Lists" → your saved list
3
Tap the attachment (📎) icon
In the broadcast chat, tap the 📎 paperclip → "Gallery" → select the downloaded flyer image.
4
Add caption (optional) and Send
You can add a short caption text, then tap Send. All contacts in your broadcast list receive the flyer as a private individual message — just like a direct chat!
WhatsApp Web alternative: Open web.whatsapp.com, open your broadcast, click the 📎 attachment icon, select the downloaded flyer file, and send.
WhatsApp Broadcast keeps each message private — recipients do NOT see each other.

How to Attach & Send Image Flyers in WhatsApp
Method 1: 1-Click Send (Interactive)
1 Click "Send" on any contact in the queue.
2 The flyer image is automatically copied to your clipboard.
3 In WhatsApp Web, press Ctrl + V (or Right Click → Paste) and hit Send!
