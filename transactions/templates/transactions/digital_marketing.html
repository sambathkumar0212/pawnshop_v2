How to Attach & Send Image Flyers in WhatsApp
Method 1: 1-Click Send (Interactive)
1 Click "Send" on any contact in the queue.
2 The flyer image is automatically copied to your clipboard.
3 In WhatsApp Web, press Ctrl + V (or Right Click → Paste) and hit Send!
Tamil (தமிழ்): வாட்ஸ்அப் திறந்தவுடன், படத்தை இணைக்க உங்கள் கீபோர்டில் [ Ctrl + V ] அழுத்தினால் போதும்.
Method 2: Auto-Broadcast (PyWhatKit)
1 Click the "Auto-Broadcast (PyWhatKit)" button at top right.
2 Select "Send Image Flyer + Personalized Message".
3 Python automatically opens WhatsApp Web, attaches the image file, and sends to each contact in sequence!
100% hands-free image attachment with zero manual pasting needed.
