Tiered Interest Rate Structure Details
How it works: Define different interest rates for three periods of the loan:
  1. Early Period: Repayment within the early period gets a reduced interest rate.
  2. Standard Period: Repayment within early + standard period gets the base gold interest rate.
  3. Late Period: Repayment after standard period gets the late period rate.

Scheme Settings Guide
Creating Gold Loan Schemes

Gold loan schemes use monthly interest rates per Rs: 100 of loan amount. The system will automatically calculate the annual interest rate.

Field Explanations
  • Gold Interest Rate: Monthly interest in rupees per Rs: 100
  • Expiry Period: Loan term in months
  • Processing Fee: Percentage of loan amount charged upfront
  • Amount Range: Min and max loan amount allowed
  • Special Conditions: Any additional terms or rules
Example

A gold loan scheme with interest rate of 1.00 means Rs: 1 per Rs: 100 per month, which equals 12% annual interest.
